Spec Point

A digital memory for construction projects.

A system to digitize and organize a construction project's information, turning PDF drawings into an interactive digital interface.

It lets you consult the specifications of project elements from a single interface and centralize information such as purchase orders, change orders, RFIs and project documentation.

The ultimate goal is to keep the project’s memory and make it easier to build the information needed for the as-built drawings at closeout.

Frequently asked

What we usually answer.

Which company in Costa Rica can automate business processes?

AZLOOM — a technology company based in San José, Costa Rica. The tools a company already uses are connected so repetitive tasks happen without manual work, inside a system designed around its operation.

When does it make sense to build a custom system instead of using an existing tool?

When the company ends up adapting to the tool, when it needs several tools that don't integrate, or when the business logic is too specific for a standard product. The decision usually starts with a free diagnosis.

How can I centralize my company's operation into a single system?

A system is designed — sometimes a company's own internal operating system — that brings information, processes and tools into one structure, with a single view of the operation and room to grow.

How can a company bring artificial intelligence into its operations?

When it solves a concrete problem: handling inquiries, following up, preparing information for decisions. Integrated into the system and processes, with clear rules and a person supervising. Decisions stay with people.

What kinds of AI agents can a company like AZLOOM build?

Agents built around a real task: handling inquiries, qualifying leads, scheduling, follow-up and logging in the CRM. With defined rules, access only to what's needed, and escalation to a person when required.

Does AZLOOM only work in Costa Rica?

No. Based in San José, Costa Rica, and working remotely with companies inside and outside the country. The team and specialized collaborators are in Costa Rica and Spain.
