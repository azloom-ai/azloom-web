Systems

The systems we build.

An AZLOOM system is built from these five capabilities. They aren't sold separately — they're combined around what your operation needs, and they almost always start with one and grow from there.

AI, automation, software and data are the tools we use to build each system. The tool is never the goal — a working operation is.

01

Process automation

What it is

We connect the tools you already use and make the repetitive work happen on its own.

What it includes

Moving data between systems, automatic follow-ups, report generation and delivery, reminders and alerts, cross-team workflows.

When it makes sense

When your team spends hours each week moving information around or on tasks that always follow the same steps.

02

AI agents

What it is

Assistants that respond and follow up on your channels, with clear rules and a person in charge of supervising them.

What it includes

Handling frequent questions, qualifying leads, scheduling, follow-up and logging in your CRM, and escalation to a person when needed.

When it makes sense

When more inquiries arrive than the team can answer in time, or when follow-up slips through.

03

Custom apps, ERP and operating systems

What it is

Platforms built around how you work, bringing the operation into one place.

What it includes

Client and project management, team and tasks, inventory or suppliers, finances, and the modules specific to your business.

When it makes sense

When the operation lives scattered across many spreadsheets and tools that don't talk to each other.

04

Systems audit and strategy

What it is

We analyze how your company works today and define what to build, in what order, and with what priority.

What it includes

A map of current processes and tools, the points where time or information is lost, and a staged system proposal.

When it makes sense

At the start of almost any project, or when you know something isn't working but not where to start.

05

Dashboards and data

What it is

We bring your scattered information into clear panels so you decide with data, not memory.

What it includes

Connecting your data sources, key indicators per area, views for leadership and for teams, and automatic reports.

When it makes sense

When every question about the business means asking someone for a file.
