Projects

Systems designed to resolve complex operations.

A few of our custom projects for companies.

01

Suppliers

A system to centralize supplier management on one platform, including supplier profiles, invoices, purchase orders, change orders and project selection.

Each supplier has their own profile and access, while the company keeps a centralized view of the operation and its projects.

02

Real Estate

An operating system to centralize a real estate company's operation in one place.

It includes a custom agent integrated into the system.

03

Clean Energy

An operating system designed to centralize the operation of a clean-energy company in one place.

It includes a custom agent.

04

Spec Point

A digital memory for construction projects.

A system to digitize and organize a construction project's information, turning PDF drawings into an interactive digital interface.

It lets you consult the specifications of project elements from a single interface and centralize information such as purchase orders, change orders, RFIs and project documentation.

The ultimate goal is to keep the project’s memory and make it easier to build the information needed for the as-built drawings at closeout.
